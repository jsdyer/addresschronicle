From a match to a useful finding

  1. Search the street first, then add the number or surname.
  2. Open the scanned page. OCR may join columns or misread a digit.
  3. Check the city, edition year and printed page.
  4. Save the evidence and compare other available editions.

Street names and numbering change. A directory entry is a historical claim made by that publication; no match is not proof that someone was absent.

Your research stays in your browser

Queries stay in memory. Only evidence you explicitly save is stored locally. Opening a scan contacts Internet Archive; it may use its own cookies. No account or API key is needed.
